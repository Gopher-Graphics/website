We meet every Wednesday in Keller Hall 3-230.

Each year we host a LAN Party, where we meet up with other clubs and play LAN and retro videogames, and a Demo Party, where we show off the graphics demos we've made over the year.

If you come to an event, remember to fill out the [attendance form](@/here.md).

We also have a full page view of our [event calendar](https://calendar.google.com/calendar/u/0/newembed?src=c_fcb836a06aa779a6b8e378ce745bdb8b770de6328c4b52c26a40f16fcb3eaa56@group.calendar.google.com).
